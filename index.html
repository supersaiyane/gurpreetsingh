About

As a Vice President - SRE & Cloud Engineering at NatWest Group, I work with large-scale initiatives that strengthen reliability, observability, automation, and cloud modernization across mission-critical financial platforms. With deep, hands-on expertise in SRE, AIOps, FinOps, Cloud Architecture, Kubernetes, and modern DevSecOps, I focus on building systems that are secure, scalable, cost-efficient, and resilient at enterprise and regulatory scale.

My work centres on transforming complex, distributed environments into self-healing, autonomous, predictable platforms by driving SRE & Reliability Engineering, AIOps & Intelligent Automation, FinOps & Cloud Efficiency, End-to-End Observability, Modern Cloud Architecture, and Zero-Downtime Deployments.

I'm passionate about engineering excellence, maturing operational reliability, and fostering a culture where decisions are guided by data, automation, and strong SRE principles. I regularly share insights on SRE, AIOps, Observability, Kubernetes, FinOps, and MLOps, and enjoy mentoring teams as they evolve into high-performing, reliability-driven engineering organizations.


What I'm doing right now

Leading SRE & Platform Engineering at NatWest Group - driving availability, resilience, and operational excellence across mission-critical banking platforms. Also running Engineering Blueprints, a technical leadership platform focused on modern infrastructure and reliability engineering.
